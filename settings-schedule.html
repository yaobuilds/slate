Slate reads a dedicated Google Calendar on this Mac. Edit events in Google Calendar or through Grokbot. Dashboard and the calendar views refresh about every two minutes.

Guest mode shows Busy instead of titles. Choose Show all on Dashboard or the calendar page to see private details. On Wi‑Fi, that needs the PIN once per session.

To connect: add calendarId to settings.local.json on this Mac, and store Google OAuth values in Keychain under the service Slate Calendar (client-id, client-secret, refresh-token). Until then, Dashboard shows a connection reminder.

Earlier Today, Deadlines, and Money entries may still exist in data.local.json as an unused backup. Slate does not edit them here anymore.
